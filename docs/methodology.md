# Methodology

## Scope

This document describes the **portfolio demonstration methodology**, not the confidential methodology or internal documentation of the March 2025 Save the Children assessment.

## Workflow

1. Define the assessment questions and stakeholder groups.
2. Organize FGD and KII records by method, community and participant group.
3. Preserve the source language and create an English transcription workflow.
4. Review synthetic excerpts for meaning.
5. Assign initial descriptive codes.
6. Group related codes into broader themes.
7. Compare themes across participant groups and methods.
8. Identify recurring issues and contrasting perspectives.
9. Link proposed recommendations to coded evidence.
10. Report limitations and maintain a clear separation between evidence and interpretation.

## Analytical principle

A recommendation should be traceable to one or more coded pieces of evidence. In a real assessment, the analyst would also document the sampling approach, consent/safeguarding process, interviewer/reflexivity considerations and any limitations affecting interpretation.
